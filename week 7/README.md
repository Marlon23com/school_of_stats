# Week 7 - Contact Data Cleaning and Spreadsheet Analysis

## Overview
This week involved working with a contact dataset in CSV and Excel formats. The files suggest a practical spreadsheet exercise focused on organising, cleaning, and preparing contact information for reporting or operational use.

## Main files
- `week7_contacts.csv` - cleaned contact data in CSV format
- `week7_contacts.csv.xlsx` - Excel workbook version
- `week7_contacts(AutoRecovered).xlsx` - recovered workbook copy

## What the project covers
- Cleaning and validating contact records
- Handling spreadsheet formatting issues
- Preparing a structured dataset for use in reporting
- Reviewing contact information for consistency and completeness

## Learning focus
This week strengthens spreadsheet and data-cleaning skills, especially when working with real-world data that needs structure, consistency, and validation.
